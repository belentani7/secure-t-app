# Fundamentals of Artificial Intelligence in Cybersecurity

## Course Overview
This course provides comprehensive training in Artificial Intelligence applications for cybersecurity, covering:
- AI and Data Analysis foundations
- Generative AI basics
- AI in Cybersecurity applications
- Deepfakes and phishing detection
- Legal aspects of AI
- Practical workshops and real-world cases

## Course Structure

### Module 1: AI and Data Analysis
- Statistical foundations and data science applied to AI
- Data preprocessing and feature engineering
- Exploratory data analysis techniques

### Module 2: Generative AI
- Introduction to Generative AI models
- Text generation and creative applications
- Image and video synthesis
- Applications in cybersecurity

### Module 3: AI in Cybersecurity
- Threat detection using AI
- Behavioral analysis and anomaly detection
- AI-powered defense systems
- Offensive AI (deepfakes, phishing automation)
- AI audit and model validation

### Module 4: Practical Workshops
- Real-world case studies
- Hands-on workshops with AI tools
- Development of practical AI security solutions
- Ethical and privacy considerations

## Learning Outcomes
- Understand AI/ML fundamentals applied to cybersecurity
- Design and implement AI-powered security solutions
- Evaluate AI models for security applications
- Address ethical and privacy concerns
- Develop practical AI security tools

## Assessment
- Practical workshops and projects
- Quiz assessments after each module
- Final project: AI security tool development
- Certificate of Attendance (optional: Certificate of Completion)

## Recommended Background
- Basic statistics knowledge
- Introductory programming (Python)
- Fundamental cybersecurity concepts
